CARDBOARD KINGDOM - how to play
================================

Windows:  double-click  play.bat
macOS:    double-click  play.command   (or open index.html)
Linux:    double-click  play.sh        (or run: bash play.sh)
Anywhere: open index.html, or cardboard-kingdom-single-file.html, in any web browser.

No install and no internet needed. Your progress saves in the browser.

Controls
  Move ............ Arrow keys or WASD (or click / tap where to walk)
  Talk / confirm .. Z, Space or Enter
  Menu ............ Esc
  Sound on/off .... M

2-player co-op (one keyboard): choose "New Game (2 players)"
  Player 1 (Sam) .... WASD to move, Space to act
  Player 2 (Jules) .. Arrow keys to move, Enter to act
  In battles, P1 controls Sam + Sir Corrugate and P2 controls Jules + Pip.
