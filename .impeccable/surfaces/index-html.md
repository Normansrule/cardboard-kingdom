# Surface brief: landing site (index.html) and the game's interface (play.html)

Mode: Persuade for the landing page; Operate for the in-game interface. Visitors are cozy-game players and burnt-out students arriving from a shared link; the action is Play in the browser.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The Kingdom Post. The site is a postcard from Blossom Hollow asking you to come and help, and every chapter is a stamp. Refuses the cozy-indie default of a big trailer hero over a row of feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: Airmail-blue desk (navy at night) with cream postcards, airmail red/blue borders, perforated stamps holding real screenshots, violet postmarks and cancellation waves. Bagel Fat One for headlines and stamps, Nunito for reading. Ink is deep violet-navy, never black.
STORY: Visitors learn it is a free browser game about fixing a flooded cardboard kingdom with real engineering, try a tiny dam puzzle on the back of a postcard, and press Play.
FIRST VIEWPORT: One large postcard, message side up, tilted 1.6 degrees, 70% width; headline "Wish you were here. Bring a hard hat." on the left half; stamp and postmark top right; address lines and the violet Play button on the right half; a real "after" screenshot tucked behind at the right edge; the chapter stamp sheet peeks at the bottom.
