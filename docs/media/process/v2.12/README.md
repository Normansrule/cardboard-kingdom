Every image is a capture from the real game while it was being built, in order.

Chapter 12 then takes them to a marsh where the fish are disappearing, with a new puzzle family from the [design direction](../../../DESIGN_DIRECTION.md): putting the bends back into a straightened river.

## 5. The puzzle: give the brook its bends back

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| ![](15_bends_puzzle_straight.jpg) | ![](16_bends_puzzle_too_straight.jpg) | ![](17_bends_puzzle_designed.jpg) |
| The brook as the Society left it: dead straight. Rules: at least 4 bends, through both deep pools, around the houses, out at the arrow, one square sideways per row. | Testing the straight canal: "Only 0 bends. The water still rushes straight through and floods Lilypad Row." | Bending it through both pools. |

![solved](18_bends_puzzle_solved.jpg)

"6 bends. The water slows down, fills the pools, spreads into the reeds, and Lilypad Row stays dry." The real idea: straightened rivers push floods downstream faster, while bends, pools and reed beds slow water down and give fish somewhere to rest.
