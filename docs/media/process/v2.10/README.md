# How v2.10 "The Ferryyard" was built

Every image is a capture from the real game while it was being built, in order.

**This round:** a cozy graphics pass in the new style direction (Costume Quest + Animal Crossing), and Chapter 11, which brings in two more friends from the [design direction](../../../DESIGN_DIRECTION.md): **Mallow**, a horned ferryperson, and **Ibis**, a nervous archivist.

## 2. Chapter 11: blocking out the Ferryyard

| Blockout | First pass | First pass, road |
|---|---|---|
| ![](09_ferryyard_blockout.jpg) | ![](10_ferryyard_first_pass.jpg) | ![](11_ferryyard_first_pass_road.jpg) |
| Grid and solid tiles: a high road at the top, a ledge with one gap (the stairs), a beach of stilt houses, and the river. | Two problems. The ledge was a row of grey boulders that didn't read as a cliff, and every object on the beach left a square of grass on the sand. | The road with Pell, a bench and the sign: "Not on the Royal Map. ![second pass](12_ferryyard_second_pass.jpg)

**Second pass:** the ledge is now an Animal Crossing-style cliff (grass lip, corrugated cardboard face, flowers on top). Sand stays sand under objects. Cozy clutter went in: washing lines between the stilt houses, flower beds, upturned boats and a cooking pot steaming on the beach. Every door still has a red DENIED stamp.

## 3. The new cast

![cast](13_cast_sheet_first_pass.jpg)

- **Mallow**: one of the "monster folk" from the Brinemarsh. Big, shaggy, teal, with curling horns, a sailor cap, a life vest, a paddle, and a necklace of terrible souvenir spoons ("this one says I SURVIVED THE GORGE and the bowl is on backwards").
- **Ibis**: a magpie in a moss-green cardigan with round glasses and an armful of maps. She was the Silver Quill's archivist. She doesn't peck. She files.
- **Grandpa Wick**: seventy years on this beach, thirty of them rolling, in a cardboard wheelchair. "Folks keep building me stairs. Kind of them. Useless, but kind."
- **The Approval Engine**: a Society machine left running for twenty years: white card, identical folds, a giant rubber stamp, and Form Folds that walk.
